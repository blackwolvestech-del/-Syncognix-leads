# Step 4 — Business analysis and lead qualification

Step 4 turns a business (and its decision maker, when one was found) into a qualified lead:
what was observed on its website, where the visible room for improvement is, which services
that evidence supports, and a final Qualified Lead Score.

```text
Find Business                   (Step 2, OpenStreetMap)
        ↓
Prospect Score                  (Step 3, from listing data)
        ↓
Find Decision Maker             (Step 3, Prospeo — on request)
        ↓
Analyze Business                (only after "Analyze Business" / "Reanalyze")
        ↓
Fetch homepage                  (server-side, one request, SSRF-safe)
        ↓
Technical · SEO · Conversion · Local signals
        ↓
Opportunities → Recommended services
        ↓
Qualified Lead Score            (stored in Supabase and reused)
```

## Principles

- **Free.** No API key and no paid service: the homepage is fetched directly and analyzed locally
  with rules. Nothing is generated by an AI model.
- **Manual.** A website is only requested after a click on *Analyze Business*, *Analyze Selected*,
  *Analyze Remaining* or *Reanalyze*. Searching and opening pages never fetch a website.
- **Evidence only.** Every strength, opportunity and recommendation states what was (or wasn't)
  found in the fetched HTML or the listing. Nothing judges design, age or quality.
- **Unmeasured is not zero.** A score that can't be measured is `null` and shown as "Not measured".
- **Two scores, two meanings.** The *Prospect Score* (Step 3) is suitability before analysis. The
  *Qualified Lead Score* is the final score after contact data and analysis. Neither replaces the
  other.

## Setup

Run `supabase/migrations/20261007000000_business_analyses.sql` in the Supabase SQL Editor. It is
additive: it creates `business_analyses` (with indexes and Row Level Security) and changes nothing
else.

Optional, in `.env.local`: `BUSINESS_ANALYSIS_CACHE_DAYS` (default `30`).

## What is and isn't measured

OpenStreetMap has no ratings or reviews, so these are always reported as not available and are
never part of a score: Google rating, review count, search rankings, keyword positions, search
volume, Core Web Vitals / real page speed, real mobile usability (only "responsive configuration
detected"), and name/address/phone consistency across other sites.

Only the homepage is fetched. Other pages are detected from navigation links but never requested,
and forms are inspected in the HTML but never submitted.

## Layout

```text

  url-safety.ts           which URLs and IP addresses may be requested
  website-fetcher.ts      safe fetch: checked DNS, manual redirects, timeout, size cap, robots.txt
  html-parser.ts          forgiving HTML tokenizer (no dependency)
  page-analyzer.ts        facts from the HTML: technical, SEO, conversion, contact, local, social, pages
  score-engine.ts         factor lists and 0–100 scores
  findings.ts             strengths and opportunities, with their evidence and flags
  service-recommender.ts  services justified by the observed opportunities
  analyze-business.ts     combines page + listing + contact data; Qualified Lead Score
  cache.ts                Supabase reads and writes
  service.ts              cache → fetch → analyze → store (the only place a website is requested)
  filters.ts              score sorting and filtering, shared by browser and server

```

## Fetching safely

The website address reaches the server from the browser, so it is treated as hostile input.

- `http:` / `https:` only; no credentials in the URL; default ports only.
- Blocked: `localhost`, single-label and internal host names (`.local`, `.internal`, …), loopback,
  private ranges (`10/8`, `172.16/12`, `192.168/16`), link-local and cloud metadata
  (`169.254/16`, `fd00:ec2::254`), carrier-grade NAT, multicast, reserved ranges and their IPv6
  equivalents. Disguised forms (`0x7f.1`, `2130706433`, `::ffff:127.0.0.1`) are normalized first.
- The check runs inside the socket's own DNS lookup, so the address that was validated is the
  address that is connected to. A host name that resolves to a private address is refused.
- Redirects are followed by hand (max 5) and every target is validated the same way.
- 12s per request, 20s in total, and at most 2 MB of HTML is read (larger pages are analyzed from
  their start and marked as such).
- Politeness: one homepage request per analysis, a clear `User-Agent`, `robots.txt` honored, at
  most 3 website requests at once per server process. Logins, bot checks and CAPTCHAs are never
  worked around: a site that answers 401/403/429 or a challenge is reported as
  "Website analysis unavailable".

## Scores

Each score is a list of factors (`earned / max`); unmeasured factors are left out and the rest
rescaled. All weights live in `ANALYSIS_CONFIG.weights`.

| Score | From |
| --- | --- |
| Website | Technical 25 · SEO 25 · Conversion 25 · Local signals on site 15 · Content/navigation 10 |
| SEO Fundamentals | Title 15 · Meta description 10 · H1 15 · Headings 10 · Canonical 5 · Image alt 10 · Local/service relevance 15 · Structured data 10 · Internal links 10 |
| Conversion Readiness | Primary CTA 25 · Phone CTA 15 · Contact form 20 · Quote/booking option 15 · Contact info 10 · Service clarity 10 · Trust/social 5 |
| Local Presence | Listing: phone 15 · street address 15 · city+state 10 · own website 15. Website: city 12 · state 5 · phone 8 · address 8 · local schema 7 · service-area language 5 |
| Opportunity | Visible room for improvement: 40% website gap · 30% conversion gap · 30% SEO gap. No website: 90; third-party page only: 80 |
| Qualified Lead | Prospect Score 25% · Decision maker 15% · Contact 10% · Website opportunity 20% · SEO opportunity 10% · Conversion opportunity 10% · Local strength 10% |

Conversion checks depend on the category (`categoryProfile` in `config.ts`): a plumber is checked
for a quote option, a dentist for booking, a restaurant for ordering/reservations, and a car wash
is not marked down for having no form.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `complete` | The homepage was fetched and analyzed |
| `partial` | No own website (or only a social/directory page), or the HTML had no readable content. Business data is still analyzed |
| `website_unavailable` | A website is listed but couldn't be fetched (DNS, timeout, SSL, error page, access declined, robots.txt…) |

A failed fetch never scores a business as bad: website-dependent scores become `null` and the
Qualified Lead Score is computed from what is known.

## Cache

One row per user per business in `business_analyses`. A stored analysis is returned without any
website request while it is younger than the cache period (30 days; 1 day for
`website_unavailable`, since the site may just have been down) and was made with the current
`ANALYSIS_CONFIG.version`. *Reanalyze* always fetches again.

When a decision maker or email is found (or verified) after the analysis, the stored analysis is
re-qualified from stored data — the Qualified Lead Score updates and the website is not fetched.

## API

`POST /api/analysis/business` `{ business, reanalyze?, refresh? }` — signed-in users only, rate
limited per user. Request problems are plain JSON with an error status. Otherwise the response is
NDJSON: a `{"stage": …}` line as each step really starts, then one
`{"success": true, analysis, cached}` or `{"success": false, error, code, fatal}` line.

Bulk analysis calls this route once per business, three at a time, so progress is real and one
failure never affects the others.

## Prepared for outreach (Step 6)

Each stored analysis carries structured data that outreach generation can use later:
`opportunities` (pain points with evidence and a suggested action), `recommendedServices` (with
reasons and the flags that justify them), `topOpportunity`, `flags`, `strengths` and `contact`.
No outreach text is generated in this step.

## Changing the rules

- Weights, thresholds, limits, cache period, concurrency: `ANALYSIS_CONFIG`.
- Service names: `SERVICE_LABELS`. Recommendation rules: `service-recommender.ts`.
- Category expectations and service terms: `CATEGORY_PROFILES`.
- After changing how scores are calculated, bump `ANALYSIS_CONFIG.version` so stored analyses are
  redone the next time they are requested.
